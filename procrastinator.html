HOW TO IMPROVE PROCRASTINATION

HOW TO OVERCOME PROCRASTINATION — 4 EVIDENCE-BASED HABITS TO OVERCOME DELAY

TIP 01

Start with one small task.

The hardest part of any overwhelming task is the friction of beginning. Lower the entry barrier by committing to just 5 minutes of low-stakes work. Once momentum is triggered, dopamine shifts in your favor and deep engagement follows naturally.

TIP 02

Break deadlines into mini-deadlines.

Vague, monolithic deadlines invite procrastination because the brain cannot visualize the execution roadmap. Dissect big assignments into clear, granular checkpoints due over consecutive days to replace eleventh-hour panic with steady progress.

TIP 03

Use a timer to begin working now.

Adopt the Pomodoro technique: set a countdown timer for 25 minutes of single-minded focus followed by a 5-minute break. A ticking clock creates a structured, finite boundary that makes hard work feel temporary and manageable.

TIP 04

Finish important tasks before entertainment.

Practice the 'Premack Principle' by pairing high-priority obligations before digital leisure or social media scrolling. Earning leisure through completed milestones eliminates guilt and turns rest into true mental rejuvenation.
