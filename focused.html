Deep Work Principles

HOW TO IMPROVE FOCUS

Practical deep work principles for university students seeking sustained attention.

01

01 — DEFINE ONE CLEAR GOAL

Single-task intentionally — close irrelevant browser tabs. Write down a single, concrete outcome before each work block so your brain stays locked in.

02

02 — PROTECT DEEP WORK HOURS

Practice short attention intervals without checking your phone. Schedule deep work during your peak alert hours and protect that time fiercely.

03

03 — TAKE RESTORATIVE BREAKS

Take genuine cognitive rests (walks, stretching) between study blocks. Step away from screens during breaks to recharge mental stamina.

04

04 — NOTICE & RECENTER

Notice when your mind wanders and gently bring it back. Track completed tasks to build steady momentum and reinforce attention stamina.
